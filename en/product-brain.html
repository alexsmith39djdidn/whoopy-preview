How it comes into a company

Nobody hands over control on day one, and they're right not to. So the brain takes the wheel in steps, proving itself at each one:

1 · AdviserThe brain sees the whole company and proposes decisions. You still decide.
2 · DepartmentsThe brain takes on areas — planning, finance, promotion.
3 · Full control insideThe brain runs the internal management, and you go play your own football.

For the first two or three months, we run the brain and grow it. After that, it works on its own.
