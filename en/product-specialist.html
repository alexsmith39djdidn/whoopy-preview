What it is

An AI specialist is an employee responsible for one area, and it closes its tasks all the way. It doesn't suggest what to do or explain how to pay for an order: it works out the delivery, issues the invoice, checks the payment and logs the order. On its own, with no person in the middle.

It carries a task from start to finish and doesn't hand it back to you halfway: no draft to approve, no waiting for the next step, no asking what to do now. You hear about the work once it's done.

How it's built

  • Workspulls its own tools by itself, from the first message to logging the order in the database — with no person in the chain
  • Learnsreviews its own work and adjusts its rules from it. Pudra's rule of "don't message anyone who's complained about spam" was one the bot proposed itself — we looked it over and kept it. It doesn't change rules quietly: it shows them to you first
  • Remembers what it doesremembers what was decided yesterday, and why it was decided that way. Memory builds up day by day and never resets, so the area doesn't start over every morning
You don't have to keep an eye on it. One area, one memory, its own hands — it answers for its area the same way a person in that job would, and just as little needs someone standing over it.

Where it works

Inside the companyruns a department: content, automation, bookkeeping, legal — under the second brain
Out fronttalks to customers: takes orders, answers questions, sees things through to payment

The only difference is which way it's facing. It's built the same either way — its own memory, its own area, its own rules of work and its own set of tools.

  • Orderstakes the order, checks the details, works out delivery and takes payment. The customer isn't waiting for a free pair of hands
  • Shiftsputs people on shifts and makes sure no gaps are left in the schedule
  • Hiringtalks to candidates, asks the questions that matter and brings the owner a result — not a stack of applications. How this went for one venue

What it doesn't do

  • Doesn't make things upif the answer isn't in what it's been given, it doesn't invent one — it hands the conversation to a person, with the whole exchange attached
  • Doesn't decide for youprices, discounts, terms and rules are yours to set. It works within them and only changes them on your word
  • Doesn't go in overnightit's built for the task: tools, rules, memory, testing. A finished specialist is weeks of work, not an on switch

What it costs

Every specialist is priced separately: it's made for your task, not taken off a shelf. We name the price in conversation, once it's clear what exactly it will be running.

At its best under a brain

A specialist works on its own, but it comes into its own under a second brain: the brain holds the whole picture and the plan, sets the tasks and takes in what's done, while the specialist runs its area inside that plan. Once there are several areas, that's the only way they stop meeting in your head.
